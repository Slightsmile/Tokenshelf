TokenShelf is an initiative to make free access to AI tools easier to discover and use.

## What this is about

Free AI credits, token allowances, and model-access programs can be genuinely useful, but the information is often scattered, incomplete, or difficult to compare. TokenShelf brings those offers into one clear directory so people can quickly understand what is available, who it is for, and how to claim it.

The focus is practical access: helping students, independent builders, developers, and curious learners find a useful starting point without needing to search across provider announcements, documentation, and community posts.

## Our approach

- Present offers with their key conditions and claim steps in plain language.
- Keep provider-confirmed details distinct from community-submitted reward claims.
- Put useful information ahead of hype, fabricated statistics, or decorative features.
- Make discovery, browsing, filtering, and saving straightforward and accessible.

TokenShelf is built around a simple idea: access to AI should be easier to understand, not harder to find.
