# TokenShelf

## Register

product

## Users

People exploring AI tools and developers comparing free API access. Their main task is to find a useful offer, understand its conditions, and follow the instructions to claim it.

## Product Purpose

A small, useful directory of free AI credits, token allowances, and model access. The homepage introduces the collection with a strong hero, followed by featured offers, recently checked offers, and browsing by category. Individual offer pages carry the detailed information.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, clear. Modern and friendly, with concise language and an emphasis on useful facts.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated landing pages, oversized editorial typography, fabricated statistics, excessive badges, repeated descriptions, decorative dashboards, and controls that do not work.

## Design Principles

1. Make the reward and the next step immediately understandable.
2. Keep the homepage concise; reveal models, conditions, and claim instructions on detail pages.
3. Use a memorable hero without delaying access to featured offers.
4. Distinguish provider-confirmed details from community-submitted reward claims.
5. Keep navigation, search, filters, and saving predictable and functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, adequate contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not use color as the only way to communicate offer status.
